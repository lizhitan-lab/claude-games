Join Cil the clever little mousedeer on a road trip to Kuala Lumpur and learn 15 Malay words along the way! 🦌🚗🏙️

In Malay folk tales, Sang Kancil the mousedeer is the smallest animal in the forest, and the cleverest! In this story, Cil has a smelly morning, cooks lunch, watches a movie with friends, makes big plans and finally sets off for KL. Every new word comes with:
✅ a picture to help you remember it
✅ slow, syllable-by-syllable pronunciation (ha-pak, teng-ik…)
✅ the English meaning
✅ a fun example sentence in Malay

At the end, test yourself in the QUIZ: shout out the Malay word before the 3-2-1 countdown finishes! 🎉

Made for primary school kids (around 7 to 10 years old) learning Bahasa Melayu, especially for spelling and vocabulary practice at school.

📝 WORD LIST & EXAMPLE SENTENCES

1. hapak: stale smell of damp clothes
   Baju ini hapak! (These clothes smell musty!)
2. tengik: smell of spoiled cooking oil
   Minyak ini tengik! (This oil smells bad!)
3. masin: salty
   Air laut masin! (Sea water is salty!)
4. masam: sour
   Limau ini masam! (This lime is so sour!)
   Bonus: "muka masam" (a sour face) means a grumpy face 😝
5. menyiang ikan: to clean, scale and gut a fish before cooking
   Cil sedang menyiang ikan. (Cil is cleaning the fish.)
6. menambah sesudu kecil garam: adding a teaspoon of salt
   menambah = adding · sesudu kecil = one small spoon (teaspoon) · garam = salt
7. segan: shy, embarrassed (不好意思)
   Cil segan bila bertemu kawan baru. (Cil feels shy when meeting new friends.)
8. wayang: movie
   Jom tengok wayang! (Let's watch a movie!)
9. bercadang: plan to, intend to
   Cil bercadang pergi ke Kuala Lumpur. (Cil plans to go to Kuala Lumpur.)
10. sebab: because, reason
   Cil mahu pergi sebab KL sangat menarik. (Cil wants to go because KL is very interesting.)
11. kecuali: except
   Semua kawan ikut, kecuali Kura-kura. (All the friends are coming, except Turtle.)
12. menunjukkan: to show
   Cil menunjukkan peta kepada kawan-kawan. (Cil shows the map to his friends.)
13. kenderaan: vehicle
   Kereta, bas, motosikal dan kereta api: semua kenderaan! (Cars, buses, motorbikes and trains: they're all vehicles!)
14. air sungai menjadi cetek: the river water became shallow
   air = water · sungai = river · menjadi = became · cetek = shallow
15. melalui Kuala Lumpur: passing through Kuala Lumpur
   Kami melalui Kuala Lumpur! (We are passing through Kuala Lumpur!)

• Do the quiz twice: once today, and again tomorrow without watching the lesson first.
• Pause at 5:52 on the word list for a quick spelling test (dictation / imlak).
